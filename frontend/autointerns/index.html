Questions

Do you actually apply for me?

Yes. We find the roles, fill out the applications and write the cover letters. The one thing we ask of you is a final OK on each application before it's sent, so nothing goes out with your name on it that you haven't seen.

Does this work for my major?

Yes. We work with every major at any college: nursing, engineering, education, art, biology, policy, business and everything in between. If you're undecided, tell us what you're curious about and we'll start there.

Will you send hundreds of applications?

No, and that's on purpose. Recruiters can spot a mass blast. We'd rather send 40 good applications than 400 sloppy ones.

Do you use AI?

Yes, behind the scenes, to fill in forms and draft letters faster. Everything is checked against your real resume, and you see all of it before anything is sent.

Is this allowed?

You approve every application, and everything in it comes from your real experience. Some employers ask you to confirm you won't use AI tools during interviews. We don't help with interviews, and you should answer those questions honestly.

What happens to my information?

It's yours. We use it only for your applications, keep sensitive details encrypted and never share it with other students. Demographic questions are left as "decline to answer" unless you tell us otherwise. Ask us to delete everything at any time and we will.

Tell us where you want to work

We take on students in small groups, starting with whoever's recruiting cycle opens first. Leave your details and we'll be in touch.
