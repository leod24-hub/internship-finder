Questions students ask

Does it work for my major?

Yes. Fields run from software and healthcare to education, design, policy, trades, and research. If we have fewer listings for yours, paste a link and Lanyard will still fill the form.

Which job sites does it support?

It works with the application systems most employers use, such as Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and iCIMS. Support for more is added based on what students ask for. Coverage is listed in your dashboard.

Is automating applications allowed?

Some sites forbid automation, so Lanyard skips those and leaves them to you. It works in your own browser, you approve every application, and it never creates accounts or gets around bot checks. Check each employer's rules for anything you submit.

What happens to my data?

Your profile is used only to fill your own applications. It is never shared with other students. You can export or delete it at any time.

Will this get me an interview?

No tool can promise that. Lanyard saves you the repetitive work so you can spend your time on applications that fit.
