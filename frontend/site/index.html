We do the legwork. You make the calls.

Applications mean hours of typing the same details into different forms. We take that off your hands, and you stay in charge of what goes out.

  1. Tell us about you

    Your school, major, class year and the kinds of work you want to try.

  2. We find the openings

    We bring you postings that fit. Found one yourself? Send us the link and we'll handle it.

  3. We fill them out

    Every answer, plus a cover letter written from your real resume, ready for you to read.

  4. You give the OK

    Read it all, ask us to change anything, then approve. You can always say no.

What we'll never do

  • Create accounts, type your passwords or enter verification codes. Those stay with you.
  • Get around a site's security checks. If one comes up, we hand that step to you.
  • Make anything up. Every answer comes from your profile and resume.
  • Answer demographic questions unless you ask us to. The default is decline.
  • Send anything without your OK.

Questions students ask

Do you work with my major?

Yes. We cover everything from software and healthcare to education, design, policy, trades and research. If we have fewer openings in your field, send us a link to any posting and we'll still handle it.

Which employer sites do you work with?

Most of the ones employers use, including Greenhouse, Lever, Workday and iCIMS. If an employer uses a site we don't cover yet, we'll tell you up front.

Is this allowed?

Some sites don't allow outside help with applications, so we skip those and leave them to you. You approve every application, and we never create accounts or get around security checks. Check each employer's rules for anything you submit.

Do you use AI?

Yes, behind the scenes, to fill in forms and draft letters faster. Everything comes from your real resume, and you see every answer before anything is sent.

What happens to my information?

We use it only for your own applications and never share it with other students. Ask us to export or delete it at any time.

Can you guarantee an interview?

No one honestly can. What we can promise is that the busywork is off your plate, so your time goes into the roles and interviews that matter to you.
