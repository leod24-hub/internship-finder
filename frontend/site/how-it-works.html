Exactly how we apply for you.

You tell us about yourself once and pick the internships you want. We fill out every application and write your cover letters, then bring each one back to you. Nothing goes to an employer until you've read it and said OK.

Six steps, start to finish.

The yellow steps are yours. The rest are ours.

  1. Fill out your questionnaire You, once

    The questions every application asks, answered one time. We use your exact words everywhere they come up.

    • School, major, class year, GPA and graduation date
    • Work authorization, start date and the other yes-or-no questions
    • A few short answers in your own words: why your fields, something you're proud of, a challenge you handled
    • Your resume

  2. Pick your internships You

    We show you openings in your fields. Check the ones you want, as many at once as you like. Found one somewhere else? Paste the link and we'll handle that one too.

  3. We prepare each application Us

    For every internship you picked, we open the employer's application and fill in each field from your questionnaire. We attach your resume, write a cover letter for that specific role, and draft answers to the open questions from your own words and resume. We take a screenshot of the finished form, and we stop before the submit button.

  4. You read it and give the OK You

    It shows up under "Needs you" on your applications page. You see every answer exactly as the employer will, with anything worth a second look flagged in yellow. Edit whatever you want, then approve it, or say no.

  5. We submit it Us

    Once you approve, we send in exactly the answers you approved and save the employer's confirmation page as proof it went through. If the form changed after you reviewed it, we stop and check with you again.

  6. We keep track Us

    Every application lands on your tracker with its deadline, status and confirmation. You mark interviews and offers as they come in.

Where the applying happens: your own browser.

You add our helper to Google Chrome. It's a small extension, and it's what fills out and submits the applications you approve.

It works inside your Chrome, so employers see a normal application coming from you, and we never need your passwords. When you approve a batch, the helper opens each application in a background tab, fills it in, and sends the ones you approved. You can keep using your computer while it works.

One thing to know

  • Your computer needs to be on, with Chrome open, while it works. Close Chrome and it pauses, then picks up where it left off next time you open it.
  • The easiest way: pick and approve a batch, leave Chrome open while you study or sleep, and check your tracker after.
  • Preparing your answers and cover letters happens on our side, so those are ready for review even when your computer is off.

What the extension does in your browser.

"An extension that fills out forms" can sound like it takes over your computer. It doesn't. Here's exactly what it does, one application at a time.

  1. It waits for your approval

    The extension does nothing on its own. It only starts once you approve applications on your applications page, and it only works on those.

  2. It opens a background tab

    For each approved application, it opens the employer's application page in a new tab in your Chrome. Your other tabs stay as they are, and you can keep working.

  3. It reads the form

    It reads the question labels and answer choices on that page, the same way your browser's autofill does, to match each question to the answer you approved.

  4. It fills in your approved answers

    It types each answer into its field, picks from dropdowns, and attaches your resume and cover letter. It uses only the answers you approved for that application, nothing new.

  5. It checks, then submits

    Before pressing submit, it checks that the form still matches what you approved. If anything changed, it stops and asks you. If everything matches, it submits and saves the employer's confirmation page.

  6. It closes the tab and moves on

    The tab closes, your tracker updates, and it starts the next approved application. When the batch is done, it goes back to waiting.

What it can do

  • Open, fill in and submit applications you approved, on employer application sites.
  • Attach your resume and cover letter to those applications.
  • Save a screenshot of each finished form and confirmation page for your records.

What it can't do

  • Work on any site that isn't an employer application page. Your email, bank, social media and other tabs are off limits.
  • See or type your passwords, or sign in for you.
  • Read your browsing history, or what you do in other tabs.
  • Submit anything you didn't approve.

You can watch it, and stop it

The extension's icon in your Chrome toolbar shows what it's doing, like "Applying: 2 of 5." Click the tab to watch it fill in a form. Press Pause any time and it stops before the next step. Nothing half-finished is sent.

What Chrome asks when you add it

Chrome shows you the permissions an extension wants before you add it. Ours asks to read and change data only on the job application sites we support, not on every website. You can see it in Chrome's extension settings, turn it off, or remove it whenever you like.

When we stop and hand it to you

These steps are yours on purpose. We tell you right away and show you exactly where we stopped.

  • The site asks you to sign in or create an account.
  • It asks for a verification code from your email or phone.
  • A "prove you're human" check (a CAPTCHA) shows up.
  • A question can't be answered truthfully from what you gave us.
  • The form changed after you approved it.

What we'll never do

No matter what plan you're on.

  • Send anything without your OK.
  • Type your passwords, create accounts or enter verification codes.
  • Get around a site's security checks.
  • Make up experience, skills, grades or dates.
  • Automate sites whose rules forbid it.

Questions people ask

How long does the questionnaire take?

Most of it is quick yes-or-no and fill-in questions. The short answers in your own words take the longest, and only three of them are marked "most asked." It saves as you go, so you can stop and come back, and everything stays editable.

Do I have to keep my laptop open the whole time?

Only while applications you've approved are being sent. Approve a batch, leave Chrome open, and it works through them. If you close Chrome, it pauses and picks up again later. Preparing answers and cover letters happens on our side, so that part doesn't need your computer.

Will employers know I used a service?

Employers get a normal application from you, with answers you've read and approved. Some employers have rules about AI tools in applications. If a posting says so, follow it, and write those answers yourself.

Can I change an answer after it's sent?

No. Once an employer has your application, it's theirs. That's why you see and can edit every answer before you approve.

What if I find an internship you don't list?

Paste the link on your applications page. If it's a form we can fill, we prepare it like any other. If it's a sign-in portal, we prepare your answers and cover letter for you to use.
